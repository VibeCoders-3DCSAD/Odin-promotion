Most apps show you last month.
Budgie looks at next December.

Filipino spending isn’t flat. Electricity peaks in the summer, June brings enrollment and uniforms, and the -ber months build to Christmas. Budgie learns that rhythm from years of Philippine household spending data, then sharpens it with your own records, so the expensive months show up on your plan before they show up on your statement.

Three plans that agree with each other.

The forecast feeds a monthly plan that covers everything your money has to do at once. Essentials and obligations come first, then what’s left is split between your goals and your debts.

Budgie suggests. You approve, edit, or say no thanks.

  1. Budget

    Sized for the season you’re in.

    A June budget already makes room for enrollment. Bills follow your scheduled payments, debts get what your loans require, and Budgie shows you why.

  2. Savings

    A schedule you can actually keep.

    How much to set aside each month for an emergency fund, tuition or a trip, when you’ll get there at your current pace, and what it takes to catch up.

  3. Debt

    A payoff with a date on it.

    Loans and credit cards tracked to the payment, with what’s left, what’s next, and how many payments until you’re done.

Padala, Pag-IBIG and paluwagan aren’t edge cases here.

Budgie’s categories start from how money really moves in a Filipino household: family support and remittances, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions, billers like Meralco and Globe, and the cash, bank and e-wallet accounts you split it all across.

The fine print, in large print.

  • Android first

    Budgie is being built for Android phones.

  • You enter, it plans

    No bank or e-wallet linking. You log transactions yourself, and that’s what keeps your data in your hands.

  • Works offline

    Record spending without signal. It syncs when you’re back online.

  • Forecasts need a little history

    Forecasts and unusual-spending alerts start once you’ve logged enough to learn from.

  • Advice, not orders

    Budgie is decision support, not licensed financial advice. Every plan waits for your approval.
