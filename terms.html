01About Budgie

Budgie is a personal finance app in the Odin project, operated by the VibeCoders team. It is a thesis project of the College of Computing and Information Sciences, University of Makati, AY 2026–2027.

Budgie helps you record transactions, track accounts, bills, debts and savings goals, and review budgeting insights, forecasts and a coordinated Financial Plan. The app is in development, and features may change.

02Your financial decisions

Budgie provides information and decision support. Forecasts, financial health scores, suggested budgets and AI-generated explanations are estimates based on your records and available data. They may be incomplete or incorrect and are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

You choose whether to accept a Financial Plan and remain responsible for your decisions. Budgie does not connect to your bank, hold or transfer money, or make payments. Recording or scheduling a payment in Budgie does not pay a bill or settle a debt.

03Your account

Provide accurate registration information, keep your login details secure, and contact the team if you suspect unauthorized access. Each account belongs to one person. Do not share your account or enter another person’s financial information without their permission.

You are responsible for the information you enter and activity on your account. Do not enter bank passwords, payment card security codes or other credentials in transaction notes.

04Your records and privacy

Your financial records belong to you. You allow us to store, sync and process them to provide Budgie’s features, including balances, forecasts and plan explanations, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Budgie stores records on your device for offline use and syncs them when you are connected. Protect your device and keep separate copies of important financial records. Optional research or model improvement requires a separate choice; accepting these terms does not grant that permission.

07Exporting data and leaving

You can stop using Budgie at any time. Use Export data in the app’s settings to download your records, and Delete account to request account removal while online. Review the deletion status and any cancellation option shown in the app.

Uninstalling the app does not itself delete your server account. Export your data before requesting deletion and remove downloaded copies yourself when you no longer need them. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.
