# Landing page (index.html)

Scope: the public landing page at budgie-finances.app (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience: Filipinos broadly, on Android phones over mobile data. The thesis panel is a secondary audience. Job: understand that Budgie plans ahead for costly months, believe it, and tap Get early access (a placeholder: it must not promise a working waitlist or a date). Proof: the real app screenshots in `public/`, the thesis survey figures, and a sample forecast labelled as a sample. Constraints: everything in PRODUCT.md. The user requires a luxurious, trustworthy, clean feel, with no fintech gloss, academic jargon up front, Pinoy kitsch, or playfulness. It keeps the deep green, warm grey, logo colours, logo and pixel budgie.

Lead idea (user-chosen): "It sees December coming."

Memorable moment: the forecast rosette, where the year is engraved as a guilloche and December swells out in orange.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Budgie is engraved like something that holds value. The year's spending forecast is drawn as a guilloche rosette, so seasonal forecasting is the hero rather than a phone mockup. It refuses the fintech split hero with a phone, three feature rows and a stats band.

OWN-WORLD: Warm grey security paper (#ebe7de) with faint wave-line underprint. Deep green intaglio ink (#013220) for display text and line work. Logo green, yellow and orange appear only as engraved hairlines. Orange is reserved for costly months. The display face is the high-contrast Bodoni Moda (with italic), Manrope for body text, and Red Hat Mono for serials and microprint. Microprint rules are built from real or sample forecast figures. The pixel budgie sits in an oval watermark window. Pill buttons use green ink.

STORY: The visitor sees that the year has expensive months, that Budgie forecasts them and turns the forecast into a budget, savings and debt plan, that it was built from Filipino realities (survey figures, family obligations, remittances, SSS/Pag-IBIG), and that it is honest about its scope. Then they tap Get early access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width nav hairline. Left 40%: a mono kicker, then the Bodoni headline "It sees *December* coming." in about 92px, a 20px subline, the green pill CTA, and an Android/in-development/University of Makati line. Right 55%: a roughly 640px rosette of 12 month lobes with mono month labels outside, December in orange, the budgie in the watermark window, the serial BDG 2026 · 12 and a sample tag. A microprint strip sits at the bottom edge.

FORM: Security Print, number 3 on my ordered list. seed:6dba0812a0610319. Signature interaction: the rosette draws itself in on load, and tapping or hovering a month lobe reveals that month's sample forecast driver. Motion grammar: an engraving stroke-draw, done once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
