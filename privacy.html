Privacy Policy

Published 5 November 2026

01Who handles your information

Budgie is operated by the VibeCoders team as a thesis project of the College of Computing and Information Sciences, University of Makati, AY 2026–2027. The project team is Stefanie S. Gabion, Joaquin Luis T. Guevarra, Alexa Joanne Paula G. San Jose and Charles Nathaniel B. Togle.

For privacy requests, use the support channel listed in the app to contact the team responsible for handling your information.

02Information the app processes

  • Account information: registration and profile details, authentication information and account identifiers.
  • Financial records you enter: income, expenses, accounts, balances, categories, notes, debts, bills, scheduled payments and savings goals.
  • Planning information: onboarding answers, seasonal preferences, custom events, financial plans, forecasts, insights and alerts.
  • Privacy and service records: consent choices and their version, export and deletion requests, device or sync identifiers, timestamps and technical error logs used to operate the service.

Only enter information you are entitled to share. Avoid including another person’s identifying details or sensitive information in notes and custom event names.

03Why we use it

We use your information to authenticate your account, save and sync your records, calculate balances, generate forecasts and financial plans, explain recommendations, and handle your privacy requests. Technical records help us diagnose failures and protect the service.

Processing needed to provide the service is distinct from optional research or model improvement. Those optional uses require a separate opt-in and are off unless you choose to enable them. Accepting the terms does not opt you into research.

04Device storage and cloud services

Budgie keeps records locally on your device so you can work offline, then syncs with cloud services when you are online. Supabase provides account and cloud database services. Your hosting and service providers may process information outside the Philippines.

Anyone who can access your unlocked device may be able to see local records. Exported CSV files are separate copies under your control. Take care when saving or sharing them.

05Forecasts and AI explanations

Budgie uses your financial records and seasonal preferences to produce estimates and recommendations. These can be wrong; you review and choose whether to accept a Financial Plan. Budgie does not execute payments or make lending decisions.

When AI explanations are enabled for the service, financial plan evidence is sent to Groq to generate explanations. This can include category names, spending and allocation amounts, forecast changes, confirmed payment details, shortfalls or surplus amounts, and seasonal preferences or custom event labels. This processing provides explanations for your plan; it is separate from an optional research or model improvement opt-in.

Do not include sensitive personal information in custom labels. Service providers handle the information they receive under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

06Who receives information

We do not sell your personal information. Service providers receive information needed to host, authenticate, sync and operate Budgie, including the cloud and AI services described above. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Research participation requires separate permission explaining the intended use. This policy does not authorize public release of your identifiable financial records.

07Retention, export and deletion

Records are stored to provide your account and the features you use. You can download a CSV through Export data in settings, or request account deletion through Delete account while online. The app shows the request status and any available cancellation option.

Deletion may require processing time. This V1 policy does not promise a fixed deletion or backup expiry period. Information that must be retained for a legal obligation or an unresolved security issue may be kept for that purpose. Contact the team for details about a particular request.

Uninstalling Budgie does not delete your cloud account. Deleting your account does not remove CSV files you have downloaded or copies you have shared.

08Your privacy rights and choices

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have rights to be informed, access and correct personal data, object to processing, request erasure or blocking, and obtain a portable copy where applicable. You may also seek damages and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission, subject to applicable conditions.

Use the app’s export and deletion controls for those requests. Contact the team for access, correction, objections or withdrawal of an optional consent. Withdrawing optional permission does not undo processing already lawfully carried out.

Read the National Privacy Commission’s guide to data subject rights and the Data Privacy Act for the scope and conditions of these rights.

09This website and external services

This promotion website does not currently offer an active early-access sign-up form. Its illustrative forecasts do not require your personal financial records. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser connects to Google to retrieve them. The website host and font provider receive connection information such as your IP address and browser request details.

These legal pages do not set application cookies or run analytics scripts. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

10Security

Account authentication and access controls help protect your information, but no device or online service is completely secure. Keep your credentials private, secure your device and contact the team if you suspect unauthorized access. Do not send passwords or full financial exports in a support request unless a secure method has been agreed.

11Policy version and updates

This is Version V1, published on 5 November 2026. Future revisions will carry a new version label and publication date. We will communicate material changes through the app or this website and request renewed consent when required.
